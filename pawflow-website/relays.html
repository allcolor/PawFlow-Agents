Relays

Your real machine. Your tools. Your boundary.

Relays let agents work where files, browsers, desktops, services, and GPUs already exist—without uploading the project into a disposable agent cloud.

Connect a RelayInstall PawFlow

Why a Relay?

Keep orchestration separate from execution.

The PawFlow runtime coordinates agents and policy. A connected Relay executes explicit tools next to the workspace.

No stale copyRead the live repo or filesystem rather than an exported ZIP.
No forced migrationKeep private services, data, GPUs, and specialist tools on their existing machines.
Controlled accessChoose read-only or writable access and expose only the tool surfaces the task needs.
